Japanese Colored Silver Leaf

Vibrant jewel colored silver leaf sheets hand dipped with transparent dye.

Colored Silver leaf has been traditionally used for Japanese painting, textiles, and wall coverings.

Hand colored genuine Silver leaf - Vibrant colors, and soft tones, the hand made Silver leaf sheets are uniquely beautiful. Great for artwork, objects or even full wall and ceiling finishes.

This unusual leaf is available in a wide spectrum of jewel like colors. Japanese Colored Silver Leaf are 109 x 109mm and are made from pure Silver leaf. Each leaf is hand dipped in a dye containing clear plastics. Colored Silver Leaf are packaged loose with the interleaved paper cut to the same size as the leaf, and are sold in units of 100 and 500 leaves.

Japanese Colored Silver Leaf may vary in color slightly within each book and will fade if not sealed. A high quality UV sealer is recommended which will aid color retention and prevent oxidation along edges.

Alcohol based sealants, such as shellac, will dissolve the plastic coating and should not be used.

The clear plastic coating may react to the gilding size. Test all materials before gilding the project to ensure compatibility.
