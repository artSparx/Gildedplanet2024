Instacoll Mirror Shine

Achieve a Super Shine or Mirror Gloss Gold Leaf Finish with Ease

Instacoll is a revolutionary water-based gilding size designed for both interior and exterior applications.

Instocoll allows you to achieve a high-gloss, mirror-like finish without the labor-intensive process of Traditional Water Gilding. With Instacoll, you can create a Super Shine or Mirror Gloss effect effortlessly—no agate burnishing required.

With Instacoll Mirror Shine, you can easily replicate the luxurious appearance of traditional gilding techniques—minus the hassle. Perfect for artisans, restorers, and designers looking for a superior gilding solution!

What's great about Instacoll?

Mirror-Like Gold Leaf Finish – Achieve the brilliance of Traditional Water Gilding without an agate burnisher.

Easy Application – A straightforward two-step system that simplifies the gilding process.

Versatile Use – Perfect for both interior and exterior gilding projects.

Reactivatable Adhesion – Apply gold, silver, or metal leaf at your convenience by reactivating the surface as needed.

Instacoll Base

Instacoll Clear Base

Instacoll Clear Base Instacoll is a revolutionary system for exterior and interior gilding with Genuine Gold, Silver and Composition leaf. A Super Shine or Mirror Gloss finish is achieved without agate burnishing.
