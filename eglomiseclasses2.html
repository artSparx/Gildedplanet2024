Glass Gilding...Introduction to Verre Églomisé

IN-PERSON CLASS! Georgian Bay Conservation, Owen Sound, Ontario, Canada

The GildedPlanet.com is excited to partner with the Charles Douglas Gilding Studio to provide you with the highest quality, hands on training for the gilding arts.

Glass Gilding...Introduction to Verre Églomisé

Spring 2023 brings the return of the popular gilding workshop Glass Gilding: Introduction to Verre Églomisé to Owen Sound, Ontario by Gilder and Teacher Charles Douglas! This special One-Day Intensive Gilding Class, to be held at Georgian Bay Conservation introduces students to the art of verre églomisé with a focus on achieving a proper balance between brilliancy and bonding between the gold leaf and the glass. Explore the beautiful artistry of gilded mirrors and other gilded glass art objects and learn to gild with precious 6k white gold leaf while taking part in a demonstration with 23k gold leaf. This is a hands-on class with a focus on gilding along with general instruction on etching into the leaf and tutoring on backing up the image in oil based paints.

Gilder Charles Douglas, who began his gilding career glass gilding with the Visual Merchandising Dept. at Saks Fifth Avenue has taught in such cities as New York, Seattle, Owen Sound/Ontario, Louisville, and San Francisco while also developing an Online format through Zoom where students are guided step by step through this ancient artform.

NOTE: All materials and use of studio gilding tools are included. An optional gilding kit (not required for the class) is available from GildedPlanet.
