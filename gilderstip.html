Gilding on glass surfaces

How to gild on glass surfaces. - 'Reverse Gilding on Glass' and 'Verre Eglomise'. Applying gold leaf to glass is a technique that has been employed for centuries by sign makers, lettering specialists, and specialty mirror makers. Glass gilding is also know as 'Reverse Gilding on Glass' and 'Verre Eglomise'.
