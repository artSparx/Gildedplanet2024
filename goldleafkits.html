Genuine Gold Leaf Kits

Gold Leaf Kits for Every Skill Level – From Beginner to Professional

Whether you're just starting out or have years of experience, our Gold Leaf Kits are designed to help you create stunning, professional-quality finishes with ease.

Choose from Basic Gilding Kits for an easy introduction, or Professional Gilding Kits for more advanced, in-depth projects. Each kit lets you explore both water-based and oil-based gilding techniques, so you can find the method that best fits your style and surface.

Every kit includes:

- Book of Genuine Gold patent leaf

- Pretinted primers for optimal surface prep

- Professional adhesive size (water or oil-based)

- A gilding brush for smooth application

- Step-by-step instructions to guide you through the entire process

Perfect for gilding picture frames, small decor items, art objects, furniture accents, and sample boards. Bring the brilliance of gold to your next project—with confidence and everything you need in one kit.
