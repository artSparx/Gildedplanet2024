Imitation Gold Leaf Rolls

Color 2.5 - Made by Nazionale in Italy

Great for gilding large scale areas like walls and ceilings. Imitation Gold is also known as Dutch Metal leaf. Our rolls of leaf are made of individual squares of Imition Gold leaf. Each leaf is laid on after another with a 2mm overlap. Rolls of imitation gold can reduce application labor up to 50%. Easily create a grid pattern or brick layout with these rolls. Made with the highest quality European gold leaf. Watch our application video

Product Description

Imtation Gold Ribbon Leaf is manufactured by placing 6 1/4" square leaves onto a continuous length of waxed paper with a 2mm overlap. The rolled paper and the cardboard core are equal in width and the Imitation Gold leaf is placed inside the edge of the paper. We offer pre-cut widths of ribbon leaf. Ribbon Leaf can be trimmed to any size required. When trimmed, the Imitation Gold leaf and paper are cut to produce a clean edge of mitation Gold and paper of the exact width.

This product is available to ship immediately. All our mitation Gold Leaf products are shipped fully insured via UPS.
