Bole Polish Brush 408 - White Bristle

Essential for a Flawless Gilded Finish

The #408 White Bristle Bole Polish Brush is a specialized tool designed for one key purpose: preparing the gilder’s clay (bole) to perfection before laying gold leaf. Made in Germany with precision craftsmanship, this brush features short, densely packed natural bristles that deliver the firm, controlled pressure needed to polish the bole to a smooth, luminous surface.

Proper bole preparation is critical to achieving a flawless, high-burnish finish in traditional water gilding. This brush is the professional's choice for working the bole surface—gently compressing and smoothing the clay to ensure a strong bond and radiant reflection when the gold leaf is applied and burnished.

Key Features:

- Firm white natural bristles ideal for polishing clay surfaces

- Short, tightly set bristles provide excellent control and pressure

- Used after the clay has dried, just before gilding

- Helps eliminate micro-texture and surface dust

- Suitable for all colors of bole

- Made in Germany for exceptional quality and durability

- Available in multiple sizes for various project scales

Whether you're restoring an antique frame or creating a high-finish panel, the Bole Polish Brush #408 ensures your surface is properly prepared—giving your gold leaf the foundation it needs to truly shine.

Pro Tip: For best results, gently rub the dry bole surface with the brush in small circular motions before applying gold leaf. Clean with a dry cloth—no solvents needed.
