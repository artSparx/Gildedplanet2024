Gesso and Whiting

For Traditional Water Gilding and frame making

Gesso is a key element in the production of traditional water gilding techniques.

Gesso, composed of finely ground marble dust and animal hide glue, has been a cornerstone in traditional frame making and furniture production for centuries. This mixture creates a smooth, durable surface that is ideal for painting, gilding, or other decorative finishes. The marble dust provides a fine, absorbent texture, while the animal hide glue ensures a strong bond, making gesso perfect for intricate detailing. When applied in layers and carefully sanded, it results in a flawless, polished finish that enhances the beauty and longevity of frames and furniture, preserving their artistry and craftsmanship.
