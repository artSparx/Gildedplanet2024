Best Oil Size adhesive glues for gilding

Great for exterior and interior projects.

Produce excellent results with gold, silver, copper and metal leaf using quick dry or slow set oil size.

Oil-based gilding adhesive, also known as oil size, is a preferred choice for achieving a highly reflective, professional-quality finish when working with gold, silver, and metal leaf. Unlike water-based alternatives, oil size self-levels and is available in varying tack times, giving artisans greater control over the application process and ensuring smooth, even adhesion.

Why Choose Oil-Based Gilding Adhesive?

Superior Reflectivity & Gloss – Oil size enhances the brilliance of gold and metal leaf, producing a deeper, more lustrous finish.

Self-Leveling Formula – Designed to spread evenly without brush marks, it creates a flawless, glass-like surface ideal for mirror-finish gilding.

Versatile for Interior & Exterior Use – Highly durable and weather-resistant, oil-based gilding adhesive is ideal for architectural elements, signage, furniture, and outdoor gilding projects.

Flexible Open Time –Available in quick-set and slow-set formulas, allowing users to choose the best drying time for their specific project.

Whether you’re working on restoration, fine art, lettering, or large-scale gilding, oil-based adhesive size ensures exceptional adhesion, durability, and brilliance, making it a top choice for professional gilders and craftsmen.

Dux Quick Dry Oil Size

Dux Quick-Dry Size produces excellent results with genuine and metal leaf and is recommended for small projects or when time is limited. The Dux Quick Dry will reach tack in approx. 1 hour and will remain open for gilding for 1.5 to 2.5 hours, depending on your current humidity and temperature. The LeFranc oil size will reach tack in approx. 1.3 hour and will remain open for gilding for 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on your current humidity and temperature. In very dry and warm environments, it may reach tack in as little as 45 mins. and the open time for gilding may be greatly reduced. Allow to cure for 24 hours before sealing. LeFranc Quick Dry Oil Size

LeFranc Quick-Dry Size is suitable for interior and exterior work. It reaches tack in 1.5 hours depending on film thickness, temperature and humidity. After reaching the proper gilding tack, it will remain open for gilding with genuine gold leaf for approx. 3 hours. The open time for gilding with metal leaf is shorter, since metal leaf is brittle and heavier. Allow the gilded surface to cure one week before sealing or glazing. Always stir LeFranc size. The driers in LeFranc size settle to the bottom of the container and must be stirred to produce a blended product.
