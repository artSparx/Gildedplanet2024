Mixol Universal Tints

Highly Concentrated, Versatile Colorants for Paints & Coatings

Mixol Universal Tints are premium, highly concentrated tinting pastes designed to add rich, vibrant color to virtually any type of paint or coating material. Manufactured in Germany, Mixol is a binder-free, glycol-based tint that easily suspends in water-, oil-, and solvent-based finishes, making it one of the most versatile colorants available.

Why Choose Mixol Universal Tints?

Compatible with Almost Any Finish – Perfect for oil-based and latex paints, lacquers, shellacs, Venetian plaster, concrete floor coatings, cement, wood putty, and wood fillers.

Highly Concentrated – A little goes a long way! Mixol is so potent that it is typically measured in drops, not ounces.

Superior Mixing & Suspension – With ultra-fine particle size, Mixol seamlessly blends into any base without separation or streaking.

Long Shelf Life & Stability – Heat- and frost-resistant, Mixol will never dry out or thicken. If it temporarily thickens due to cold weather, simply warm it to room temperature, shake, and use.

Ideal for Faux Finishes & Color Corrections – Popular among faux finishers and professional painters for its ease of mixing and ability to fine-tune colors with precision.

Infinite Shelf Life – No Waste, No Drying Out

Unlike traditional tints that rely on binders, Mixol’s binder-free formula ensures an unlimited shelf life. This means no drying, no thickening, and no wasted product. Even if exposed to cold temperatures, it remains unaffected—just warm it, shake it, and it's ready to use.

Product Description

MIXOL - Mixol is a binder-free multi=purpose tinting paste - not a ready paint!

Mixol forms no skin or lumps and will not dry out. It can be stored for many years and so it is extremely economical.

Mixol is highly resistant to frost and heat, but temperatures below 5C can cause thickening. In that case it needs only to be thawed at room temperature. Then shake thouroughly and Mixol is ready for use again.

In case of tinting lacquers sometimes Mixol cannot be mixed easily by hand, therefore use a stirring apparatus, if possible, to get optimal results and to avoid rub-out effect. It is important always to test results after stirring!

This product is available to ship immediately. All our Mixol products are shipped fully insured via UPS.
