Best Top Coat Sealers for Genuine and Metal leaf gilding

High quality, professional sealers for gilding on furniture, frames, objects, walls and ceilings.

Every gilding project is different and choosing the right sealer can be challenging.

Select from oil based clear sealers, water based top coats, shellac, asphaltum, spar varnish, back up varnish and more.

Imitation Gold and Copper leaf will oxodize (tarnish) over time.Therefore, these metals require a clear coat sealer. Genuine Gold leaf, lower than 23kt, as well as Genuine Silver leaf also require clear coat sealers to prevent oxidation.

Water Based

Polyshield can be used over genuine gold, white gold, Silver, imitation gold, copper, and aluminum. Polyshield is available in gloss and satin. It is non-flammable, non-toxic and environmentally safe. DO NOT USE with patent leaf or rolls, due to the wax reside on the backing paper.

Dewaxed Blonde Shellac Flakes

Pure shellac flakes produces a light transparent colored shellac finish.

Blonde Dewaxed Shellac Flakes creates the perfect French Polish and is ideally suited as a barrier coat for gilded applications and for antique restoration on wood surfaces and furniture.

The finish dries quickly and has a luxurious feel once rubbed out. Shellac is also 100% natural and non-toxic.

Shellac in flake form allows you to mix up only as much as you need in order to minimize waste and maximize shelf life.

Production Method

- Add 250g of shellac flakes to 1 liter of methylated spirits and allow the shellac flakes to soak for a minimum of 24 hours or until all the shellac flakes have dissolved.

- Stir or shake occasionally to help the shellac flakes to dissolve more easily.

- More shellac flakes or methylated spirits may be added to adjust the strength according to your preference.

- Store in a cool, dry place.

Gilder's Asphaltum

Asphaltum is used as a darkening liquid for creating shade effects on genuine and metal leaf.

Gilders Asphaltum - 1 Pint U.S. Solvent based. Manufactured in the USA.

Asphaltum Varnish in a glaze is capable of creating a wide spectrum of colors.

- Thin coats of Asphaltum Varnish appear light gold.

- Medium coats of Asphaltum Varnish create rich Copper tones.

- Heavy coats of Asphaltum Varnish create Bronze Burnt Umber undertones.

- Very thick coats of Gilders Asphaltum Varnish appear Black and opaque.

Aspahltum is also a varnish and can be used as a topcoat. Additional topcoats are not required but depending on your project, you may choose to apply an additional sealer.

Many Antique Vintage Mirrors have Asphaltum Varnish as the original Backing Paint after the Glass was Silvered. Asphaltum Varnish was applied to add depth and scratch protection. Asphaltum is used in reverse glass sign work, in sandblasting frosted glass, glue chipping as a resist and shading effects. Gilders Asphaltum Varnish is one of the worlds oldest coating still used today.
