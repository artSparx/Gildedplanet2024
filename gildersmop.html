Mop Brushes for Gilding

Mop brushes are essential tools in the gilding process, perfect for dusting skewings from gilded surfaces. Available in a variety of sizes and shapes, these brushes cater to different gilding needs with precision.

Choose the Perfect Brush for Your Project

Soft Squirrel Hair Mops: Ideal for genuine gold leaf surfaces, these brushes are gentle enough to dust without risking scratches, preserving the delicate gold finish.

Black Goat Hair Mops: With firmer bristles, these mops are perfect for tamping and dusting metal leaf. However, they are not recommended for use on delicate genuine gold leaf due to their firmness.

Select the Right Shape for the Job

Oval-Shaped Mops: Best suited for ornate or curved surfaces, providing even coverage without missing intricate details.

Square Mops: Perfect for flat surfaces, ensuring a smooth, consistent finish across large areas.

Pointed Mops: Specifically designed for applying "gilders liquor" during traditional water gilding or the Kolner Gilding System, offering precision in tight or detailed areas.
