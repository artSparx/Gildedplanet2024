Copper Leaf Gilding Kits

Our complete tool kits with Genuine Copper leaf are designed for both beginners and professionals.

Each kit includes all the necessary materials and detailed step-by-step instructions to help you achieve beautiful gilding results. Whether you are new to gilding or looking to expand your skills, we have the perfect kit for you.

Basic Gilding Kit

Includes all the tools and materials needed to apply Copper Leaf.

This kit contains:

  • Basic step-by-step instructions
  • One book of Copper leaf (loose leaf)
  • One 4 oz. bottle of Aquasize (water-based adhesive size)
  • Natural hair brush
  • Cotton and mixing sticks
  • Wet/dry paper

Note: Clear Coat Sealer sold separately. Each book contains 25 leaves, each leaf measuring 5.5" x 5.5". Approximate coverage is 5 sq. ft.
