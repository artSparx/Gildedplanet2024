Metal Leaf Schaibin

Large quantities of irregular size Aluminum, Copper or Imitation Gold leaf.

Create beautiful broken leaf effects with Schaibin metal leaf. Large, irregular sheets of loose imitation metal leaf.

Suitable for any indoor artwork, furniture and objects. Great over plaster, stone, canvas, paper, metal and wood.

Approx. coverage for 60 gram box is 20 sqft. - 100 gram is 35 sqft. - 1 Kilo is approx. 350 - 400 sqft.
