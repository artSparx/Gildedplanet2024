Product Description

Aluminum Leaf Packs and Books.

Introducing our premium Aluminum Leaf Packs and Books, also known as Imitation Silver Leaf. These top-quality pure aluminum leaf sheets are available in three formats: loose leaf, patent leaf, and ribbon leaf rolls, catering to all your gilding needs.

Our Aluminum Leaf products are sourced from the finest European and Chinese manufacturers, ensuring exceptional quality for your artistic and professional projects. Ideal for artists, crafters, frame makers, and sculptors, our Aluminum Leaf provides a stunning metallic finish that elevates any creation.

Key Features:

100% Pure Aluminum: Non-tarnishing and does not require a protective sealer, although clear coat sealers are recommended for enhanced durability and longevity.

Versatile Formats: Available in loose leaf, patent leaf, and ribbon leaf rolls to suit various application techniques.

Generous Coverage: Each pack contains 20 books, with each book including 25 leaves, totaling 500 leaves per pack. Each leaf measures 5.5" x 5.5", providing approximately 100 sqft of coverage per pack and 5 sqft per book.
