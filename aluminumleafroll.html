Aluminum and Imitation Silver Leaf Rolls

Aluminum Leaf, also known as "Imitation Silver," is available in convenient rolls for easy application.

Available in 50m and 75m Master Rolls, these Aluminum and Imitation Silver Leaf Rolls are ideal for covering large surface areas such as walls and ceilings.

Product Details:

Ribbon Leaf Creation: Each roll is created by placing 6 1/8 inch square aluminum leaves onto a continuous length of waxed paper with a 2mm overlap. This design results in a continuous row of 6-inch aluminum squares when applied, providing a uniform and seamless finish.

Manufacturing Process: Imitation Silver Ribbon Leaf is meticulously crafted by placing 6 1/8" square leaves onto a continuous length of waxed paper with a 2mm overlap. The rolled paper and cardboard core are of equal width, with the Imitation Silver leaf perfectly aligned inside the edge of the paper.

Versatility: We offer pre-cut widths of ribbon leaf, which can be trimmed to any required size. When trimmed, both the Imitation Silver leaf and the waxed paper are cut to produce a clean, precise edge, ensuring the exact width needed for your project.

These rolls are perfect for large-scale applications, offering both efficiency and precision. Enhance your projects with the brilliance and elegance of Aluminum and Imitation Silver Leaf Rolls.

Product Description

Aluminum and Imitation Silver Leaf Rolls.

50m and 75m Master Rolls. Ideal for large area surfaces like walls and ceilings. Ribbon Leaf is created by placing 6 1/8 inch square aluminum leaves onto a continuous length of waxed paper with a 2mm overlap. Once applied, a continous row of 6 inch aluminum squares will be produced.

Imitation Silver Ribbon Leaf is manufactured by placing 6 1/8" square leaves onto a continuous length of waxed paper with a 2mm overlap. The rolled paper and the cardboard core are equal in width and the Imitation Silver leaf is placed inside the edge of the paper. We offer pre-cut widths of ribbon leaf. Ribbon Leaf can be trimmed to any size required. When trimmed, the Imitation Silver leaf and paper are cut to produce a clean edge of Imitation Silver and paper of the exact width.
